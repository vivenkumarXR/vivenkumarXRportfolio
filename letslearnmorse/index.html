Let's Learn Morse Code: Blackout

A Morse code thriller in 21 days

The whole city has gone dark. Phones are dead. The internet is gone. Only Grandpa's old radio still works, and it's beeping a message nobody can read.

Learn Morse code one letter at a time and find out who is sending it. Free, for kids and grown-ups.

About 15 minutes. Turn your sound on.

    How it works

    Listen first

    You learn each letter by its sound, the way real radio operators do. There's no chart to memorise.

    Fifteen minutes a day

    Each day brings new letters, a few quick games and the next part of the story.

    Switch the city back on

    Every day you finish lights a window. Earn up to three stars a day and keep your streak going.
